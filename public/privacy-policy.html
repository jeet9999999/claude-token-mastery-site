Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 May 2026  ·  Effective immediately

ShopDive ("we", "us", or "our"), operating at shopdive.in, is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the data you provide when you visit our website or purchase our digital products.

1. Information We Collect

When you make a purchase or contact us, we may collect:

  • Personal identification: First name, last name, email address, phone number
  • Payment information: Processed securely via Razorpay. We do not store card numbers or banking details on our servers.
  • Usage data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on site (via standard server logs and analytics).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Process and fulfill your purchase (deliver the digital product to your email)
  • Send order confirmation and download instructions
  • Respond to your support requests or enquiries
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations under Indian law

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

3. Payment Processing

All payments are processed by Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd., a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. When you pay, you are subject to Razorpay's own Privacy Policy in addition to ours. We receive only a transaction confirmation and the information you entered in our checkout form.

4. Digital Product Delivery

Upon successful payment, we will send your PDF download link to the email address you provided. Please ensure the email address is accurate. We are not responsible for non-delivery due to an incorrect email address.

5. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies necessary to provide a functional browsing experience. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. By using our site, you consent to the use of these essential cookies.

6. Data Retention

We retain your purchase records and email address for a period of 3 years in accordance with Indian tax and accounting regulations. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us (see below), subject to legal retention requirements.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (where legally permissible)
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@shopdive.in.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites (e.g., Anthropic, Razorpay). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. Please review their policies separately.

9. Children's Privacy

Our products and services are not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of our website constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Governing Law

This policy is governed by the laws of India, including the Information Technology Act, 2000, and applicable RBI guidelines for digital transactions.
